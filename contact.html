Quick answers

Most questions are answered here — faster than waiting for a reply.

Where is my order? +
You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships. You can also track it from My Account › Orders.
How do returns work? +
For hygiene reasons, beauty products can be returned only if unopened and unused within 30 days of arrival. Simply reach out via our contact form or account dashboard.
Are your products authentic? +
100% authentic. All items are directly sourced from authorised brand distributors with batch code tracking and quality checks.
Can you help me choose? +
Yes! You can take our 60-second routine quiz or consult our Beauty Journal guides written by certified estheticians.
I'm a brand — how do we work together? +
We welcome submissions from clean, verified beauty creators. Select "Partnerships" on our contact form with your brand catalog.
